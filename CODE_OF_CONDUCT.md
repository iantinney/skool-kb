# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via a
GitHub issue or private report. Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove
comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
